# STILL art direction

Mineral blue, deep navy, thin Manrope typography and one liquid sculpture. The hero is deliberately bright, quiet and asymmetrical. A custom parametric volume has thick irregular folds and a central aperture. Scroll makes it open and settle into concentric water ripples. A droplet approaches the surface and dissolves into its reaction. No video, stock meditation silhouette, particle face or autonomous chapter sequence.

One native story progress drives volume deformation, pose, droplet, secondary ripples and DOM copy. Idle time only changes subtle surface variation. Reverse scroll restores the same state. A hand painted procedural reflection map is generated locally. The geometry is spatial, but reflection is an art directed shader rather than a fluid simulation.

The lower page changes rhythm: large editorial statement, three functional ritual rows, then an uninterrupted dark practice room. The main practical action is an actual local timer. There are no claims to diagnose, treat or improve a medical condition.

Mobile uses a lower, smaller sculpture and a separate text hierarchy. Reduced motion keeps the opening frame and disables camera/shape story travel and breathing scale. CSS fallback remains available when WebGL is unavailable.

The integrator must review actual desktop/mobile frames, transitions, reverse movement, material response and all controls. Syntax checking and local assets are not a visual PASS.

## Water landscape iteration

The previous loop-shaped object and left headline composition are replaced by a wide shallow water surface. The large centred headline sits above the scene rather than beside an object. Scroll lowers a separate drop, sends a visible radial disturbance outward and raises the camera into a calmer overhead view. The same vertex identities remain throughout. Pointer input adds a real surface displacement, not a decorative cursor.

The source audit found avoidable steady work in the earlier implementation: permanent visible-scene RAF at DPR up to 1.75 and a full-screen SVG turbulence layer. These are verified costs, not proof of the user's hardware bottleneck. The new scene has four inexpensive draws, no shadow map, a smaller adaptive pixel ratio and no idle RAF. Colour, normal variation and a small procedural caustic pattern supply the water material without an environment download or postprocessing.

The integrator's actual 1425 × 891 browser review confirmed the water and composition were expressive, and identified low contrast in the supporting copy. The supporting copy and CTA were then changed to opaque dark navy, with a pale opaque backing behind the arrow control.
